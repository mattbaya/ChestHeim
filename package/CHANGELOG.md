# Changelog

## 0.4.0
- **Renamed to Echo Chests** (the chest is now the *Echo Chest*). Chests built with the earlier test versions are not carried over: the old piece no longer exists.
- **Clan-linked echo chests.** Use the Clan Key on an Echo Chest (Shift+E) and link it to your clan: it then shares its contents with every Echo Chest set to the same clan, wherever they are, and clan members can take things out of it. Echo Chests that aren't linked behave as before.

## 0.3.0
- **Clan chests.** A new craftable item, the **Clan Key** (2 Bronze, 2 Fine Wood, at a workbench). Carry it and press **Shift+E** on any chest you built: set your clan name (saved on your character) and share the chest with it. Everyone whose clan is set to the same name can open the chest; nobody else can (you always can). Press Shift+E again to stop sharing. Shared chests say so when you look at them. Anyone who wants in needs their own Clan Key once, to set their clan name; they don't need to keep it.

## 0.2.0
- **Restock.** Open your echo chest and a panel appears beside it. Set how many of some items you like to carry (add them by name, or click *Remember what I carry*), then click **Restock**: the vault tops your inventory up to those amounts. The list is kept on your character, so every echo chest uses it. Drag the panel anywhere (double-click to reset); there's an X on it to close the chest.
