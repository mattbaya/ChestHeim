# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- **Restock.** Open your vault chest and a panel appears beside it. Set how many of some items you like to carry (add them by name, or click *Remember what I carry*), then click **Restock**: the vault tops your inventory up to those amounts. The list is kept on your character, so every vault chest uses it. Drag the panel anywhere (double-click to reset); there's an X on it to close the chest.
